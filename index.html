BabyTap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

BabyTap is a baby-care tracker for parents and caregivers. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

The short version

  • Everything stays on your device. BabyTap has no account, no sign-in and no server. We (the developer) never receive your data.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to sell or share.

What you enter and where it is stored

BabyTap stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your baby's (or babies') name, date of birth and, if you choose one, a photo.
  • Care entries you log: feeds (with optional amount and side), diaper changes (with an optional stool mark) and sleep times.
  • Your app settings (theme, reminders, night mode, backup preferences).

This information is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

Photos

If you add a baby photo, you choose it with your phone's own photo picker. BabyTap only receives the single photo you select and keeps a copy in its private storage. It does not access the rest of your photo library.

Notifications

Reminders (nap timer, feed/diaper reminders, wake-window and heads-up alerts) are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Backups

  • Automatic backups are saved as files in a folder you choose on your device (Android) or in the app's folder in the Files app (iPhone). If that folder is synced by another app (for example Google Drive or OneDrive), that app's own privacy policy applies to the synced copy.
  • Export opens your phone's share menu so you decide where the backup file goes (for example email, Google Drive or a messaging app). BabyTap does not send it anywhere by itself.
  • Restore reads a backup file you pick.

On Android, the system's own device backup may also include app data in your Google account backup, under Google's policies; you can turn this off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Data → Erase all data deletes everything BabyTap stores on the device.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from the device.
  • Backup files you saved or exported are yours; delete them wherever you saved them.

Children

BabyTap is intended for adults (parents and caregivers). It is not directed at children, and it does not collect information from children. Information about a baby is entered by the adult using the app and stays on that adult's device.

Health information

BabyTap records care routines and shows gentle reminders. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you are worried about your baby's health, contact a pediatrician or other qualified health professional.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show the reminders you turn on.
  • Internet — required by the app framework; BabyTap does not send your data over the network.
  • Vibration — for gentle haptic feedback when you log something.
