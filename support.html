Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. BabyTap has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone: Settings → Backup → Export backup, and save the file (for example to Google Drive, iCloud Drive or email). On the new phone: on the first screen tap "Restore a backup", or later Settings → Backup → Restore.

How do I log a stool or a feed amount?

Press and hold the Diaper button to mark a stool change. Press and hold Milk to set the amount with a slider.

I tapped by mistake.

Tap UNDO on the message that appears. Repeat taps within a minute are ignored automatically; tap "Log again" if you really meant it. Any entry can be edited or deleted from the history.

Reminders don't arrive.

Allow notifications for BabyTap in your phone's settings. On some Android phones, also turn off battery optimisation for BabyTap so reminders aren't delayed.

How do I remove a baby or start over?

Settings → Babies → tap a baby → Remove. To delete everything: Settings → Data → Erase all data (you can save a backup first).

Is BabyTap medical advice?

No. BabyTap is a care log with gentle reminders, not a medical device. If you're worried about your baby, contact your pediatrician.
